# Sleeper Picks: mechanics, payout math, promos, legality and ToS (as of October 17, 2026)

Scope note for the writer: "Sleeper Picks" / "Player Picks" is Sleeper's daily-fantasy more/less pick'em. The single most important structural fact uncovered: as of 2026 the product is no longer an against-the-house parlay in the classic sense. Sleeper's own rules page (updated Feb 13, 2026) describes Player Picks as a peer-to-peer game in which every entry is dropped into a pool of up to 5,000 entries, and Sleeper's "Where Can I Play?" pages (Aug 3, 2026) state that the format "previously known as PicksVS" is now offered "under the Picks brand name in all available jurisdictions." The economics for a bettor are nevertheless still driven by per-pick multipliers: the "Guaranteed Contest Payout" equals entry fee x the entry's VS Score, and independent entry-screen measurements (Oct 2026) show the displayed entry multiplier is the plain product of the per-pick multipliers (Max) or a round-robin mean of sub-combinations (Flex). Many affiliate pages still describe the older "100x cap / $5,000 max win / $50 max entry" product; these are dated and conflict with Sleeper's current pages, and the conflicts are flagged below.

Primary-source files were downloaded verbatim from support.sleeper.com (the `.md` renderings of each article) on 2026-10-17; quotations below are from those files.

## Key Question 1: Exact payout formula for an N-pick entry in 2026, including caps, pick limits and entry limits

### Takeaway
Official rules: 2-8 picks per entry, each pick showing its own per-side multiplier; an entry earns a "VS Score" and is paid the higher of (a) entry fee x VS Score (guaranteed, if the required number of picks hit) or (b) 100x the entry fee for finishing 1st in a pool of up to 5,000 entries (split on ties). Sleeper publishes no formula for the VS Score and no explicit dollar cap; its App Store listing advertises "win up to 1000x real cash." Independent entry-screen measurements (Oct 2026) show the Max multiplier equals the plain product of the leg multipliers truncated to the cent (e.g., 1.79 x 1.78 = 3.1862 -> 3.18), with a small size bonus observed at 3-4 picks. Max entry fee in regular pools is $500 (official, Feb 2026); older third-party pages say $50 and a $5,000 max win, which conflict with current official text.

### Inferences
- The VS Score is, for practical modeling purposes, the entry's displayed payout multiplier: the official rule says payout = fee x VS Score and "higher-difficulty picks increase potential scoring," and the entry-screen measurements show the displayed multiplier equals the product of leg multipliers (plus a small observed size bonus at 3-4 picks in NBA, or a 2-8% haircut in one MLB sample). A modeler should use M_max = prod(m_i) as the base case and treat the ±8-10% size adjustments as unconfirmed single-source observations.
- The "pool" wrapper changes little for EV: the guaranteed payout is independent of other entrants, and the 100x 1st-place prize only matters when it exceeds fee x VS Score (i.e., for entries whose own multiplier is below 100x and which post the top VS Score among up to 5,000 entries). The clause awarding 100x to the highest VS Score when no entry is fully correct creates a small positive tail for partially-correct entries that is not modelable without pool composition data.
- The "1000x" figure is best read as the practical ceiling for an 8-pick entry (e.g., eight ~2.37x legs give 1000x), not a published cap; no official text found caps payout in dollars. The "$5,000 max win" and "$50 max entry" figures appear only in affiliate pages and conflict with Sleeper's Feb-2026 rules ($500 regular-pool max entry) and App Store copy (1000x).
- Because entry limits "vary by user and by sport" and §5.18 lets Sleeper alter entry limits and payout multipliers per account, the $500 figure should be treated as a ceiling, not an entitlement.

### Gaps
- No official document states the VS Score formula, any maximum VS Score, or a dollar cap on a single payout; I found no Sleeper statement reconciling "1000x" with the pool/VS-Score structure.
- The observed 3- and 4-pick "Max size bonus" (x1.08, x1.10) and the MLB 2-8% haircut come from one unverified GitHub project; no second source confirms either.
- No source states a minimum entry fee in the current pool format (third parties say $1; Protected Pick entries officially require a $1.00 minimum).

## Key Question 2: Does Sleeper offer a reduced-payout "insurance"-style entry, and what is its table?

### Takeaway
Yes: "Flex" contests (3+ picks) pay with one miss, and with two misses at 5+ picks, at a lower perfect-entry multiplier than Max; Sleeper publishes no table because Flex payouts are derived from the per-pick multipliers (minimum Flex multiplier 1.25x). Independent entry-screen measurements show the 3- and 4-pick Flex payout equals the round-robin mean of the (n-1)-pick sub-products, with a one-miss payout of the surviving sub-product divided by n. Separately, "Injury Insurance" voids MORE picks on players injured early, and "Protected Pick" promos refund losses as playthrough credit.

### Cited Findings
- Flex rules (article dated February 2, 2026): "With Flex contests: You can receive a payout with one incorrect player pick; You may also receive a payout with two incorrect player picks (only available for contests with five or more picks)"; "Flex payouts are only available for contests with three or more picks"; "The contest must have a minimum Flex payout multiplier of 1.25x"; "the payout multiplier for a perfect entry is lower than in Max contests." — [Sleeper Player Picks Flex Contests](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/9261402-sleeper-player-picks-flex-contests)
- Voids in Flex: "the contest will be graded using adjusted Flex payouts, as if the voided pick(s) were never included"; "If voided picks reduce the entry to two remaining picks, the contest will be treated as a 2-pick Max contest"; "If voided picks cause the entry to include players from only one team, the entire entry will be canceled and refunded." — [Sleeper Player Picks Flex Contests](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/9261402-sleeper-player-picks-flex-contests)
- CBS Sports describes the Flex threshold as "a Flex multiplier of at least 2x" (conflicts with the official 1.25x). — [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/betting/news/sleeper/)
- Empirical Flex formula (Alphadog, Oct 2026): "Flex = the round-robin mean over the (n minus 1)-pick sub-combinations; voids drop the pick"; 3-pick Flex of 1.79/1.78/1.75 displayed 3.14 vs round-robin mean 3.1446; two-of-three paid 1.05 = sub-product/3; 4-pick Flex (1.79/1.75/1.80/1.78): round robin 5.639 vs app 6.09; 3-of-4: 1.39-1.43 predicted vs app 1.52; 5-pick Flex observed 9.42 / 1.92 / 0.39 (all hit / one miss / two misses); 6-pick Flex 21.67 / 1.86 / 0.37. — [Alphadog: NBA_MULTIPLIERS.md](https://github.com/Rodantmat/Alphadog/blob/7f31da5a4498fa946be7f67069f33e16cea98a8c/nba/NBA_MULTIPLIERS.md); [Alphadog: NBA_FULL_SYSTEM_CERTIFICATION_2026-10.md](https://github.com/Rodantmat/Alphadog/blob/2a47f62f88b437d4b6373f90cb9ebf569ebf5c75/nba/NBA_FULL_SYSTEM_CERTIFICATION_2026-10.md)
- Same project's MLB verification: "Flex = a round-robin over the (n−1)-pick sub-combinations, each priced with the same per-leg product (3/3 Flex predicted 2.775× vs real 2.78×; 2/3 inside the predicted 0.884–0.963× range, real 0.92×)." — [Alphadog: NBA_FULL_SYSTEM_CERTIFICATION_2026-10.md](https://github.com/Rodantmat/Alphadog/blob/2a47f62f88b437d4b6373f90cb9ebf569ebf5c75/nba/NBA_FULL_SYSTEM_CERTIFICATION_2026-10.md)
- Injury Insurance (official): applies when a player meets sport criteria "and do[es] not return to the game." NFL: "Player leaves the field in the 1st half and does not return in the 2nd half"; "Only offensive players are eligible"; preseason excluded. NBA/WNBA: leaves in 1st half, no return (no NBA preseason). MLB batters: "leaves the game with 2 or fewer plate appearances"; pitchers: "8 or fewer outs recorded" due to injury. NHL: injured in 1st period, no return. CFB/CBB: offensive players from Top-25 matchups only. Regrading: "MORE picks that lost are voided as if the pick was never included"; "LESS picks — Graded as a win if the player's recorded stat is below the projection"; "MORE picks — Graded as a win if the player hit their projection before getting injured"; "If at least 2 player picks from multiple teams remain, the contest is graded normally with an adjusted payout multiplier; If fewer than 2 qualifying picks remain, the contest is canceled and refunded." — [Sleeper Player Picks Injury Insurance](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/8674329-sleeper-player-picks-injury-insurance); [NFL Injury Insurance](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/9869560-nfl-injury-insurance)
- Protected Pick promotions: "provide users with the opportunity to place protected contest entries in which losses are credited back in the form of play-through funds"; "Protected Pick entries must have a minimum entry fee of $1.00. You may only use non-playthrough funds"; "only eligible to place one (1) Protected Pick contest entry in any given Promotion"; entry fee capped at "each Promotion's specified dollar amount"; credited losses must be played through within 30 days. — [Protected Pick Promotions](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/6821953-protected-pick-promotions)
- Combos (Team Pick + Player Picks) are a separate product, not an insurance product: "A Combo pairs one Team Pick with a Player Picks entry that includes two or more Player Picks"; "Once you submit a Combo, it can't be canceled." — [What are Combos?](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/15396866-what-are-combos)

### Inferences
- If Flex = round-robin of (n-1)-subsets priced at the per-leg multipliers, then a 3-pick Flex has exactly the same EV as a 2-pick Max built from the same legs (it is an equal-weight portfolio of the three 2-leg parlays): EV_flex(3) = (mean p_i p_j m_i m_j); with symmetric 1.78x legs at true 50%, EV = 0.89^2 = 0.792 of stake. Flex therefore does not buy EV, only variance reduction, at 3-4 picks.
- The observed 5- and 6-pick Flex tiers (9.42/1.92/0.39 and 21.67/1.86/0.37) do not fit a single round-robin rule (e.g., 1.78^4/5 = 2.01 is close to the one-miss 1.92 but 1.78^3/10 = 0.56 is far from the two-miss 0.39), so the 5+ pick Flex formula is unknown and the two-miss tier appears priced below a simple round robin.
- Injury Insurance is asymmetric in the bettor's favor on LESS picks (graded as a win if the truncated stat is below the line) and neutral-to-favorable on MORE picks (voided rather than lost), which slightly raises EV on injury-prone players relative to a sportsbook.

### Gaps
- Sleeper publishes no Flex payout table or formula; the round-robin rule is a single-source empirical finding verified only at 3 and 4 picks (NBA and MLB); 5-8 pick Flex pricing is undocumented.
- No source quantifies how often Protected Pick / Injury Insurance regrades occur.

## Key Question 3: Can you combine picks from the same game, same team or same player? Correlation rules?

### Takeaway
Official rules: no duplicate picks on the same player; every entry must include players from at least two teams (an "ez Pick" at 1.0x that always wins is auto-added to satisfy this); same-game and same-team stacks are otherwise allowed. Sleeper's only explicit correlation block is in Combos (Team Pick + Player Picks), where "highly correlated outcomes" may be blocked. No official same-game payout discount is documented; one empirical 4-pick Max with two legs from one game paid above the plain product.

### Cited Findings
- "You may not include multiple picks for the same player." — [Sleeper Player Picks Rules](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/9047931-sleeper-player-picks-rules)
- "All contests must include players from multiple teams — If voided picks leave only one team, the entire contest is canceled and refunded." — [Sleeper Player Picks Rules](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/9047931-sleeper-player-picks-rules)
- "All Sleeper Player Picks entries must include at least one player from multiple teams. This rule applies to both Max and Flex contests." — [Sleeper Player Picks Flex Contests](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/9261402-sleeper-player-picks-flex-contests)
- In-game contests: "Select players from two or more teams"; "You can combine in-game and pre-game predictions within the same contest entry, as long as all other contest requirements are met." — [Sleeper Player Picks In-Game Contests](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/6998002-sleeper-player-picks-in-game-contests)
- ez Pick: "An ez Pick supports Player Picks entries when a slip starts with one selected player or multiple selected players from only one team"; "ez Pick is a 1.0x multiplier, so it doesn't change your payout"; "It always grades as a win"; "If a user selects just one player, an ez Pick may be automatically included from the opposing team in that game. ### Inferences
- Same-game and same-team correlation (e.g., QB pass yards + WR rec yards, or two teammates' overs) is allowed in Player Picks and, on the evidence found, not repriced at the entry level; the only structural constraints are "no same player twice" and "two or more teams." This is more permissive than Underdog's documented same-game discount and PrizePicks' adjusted Flex partials.
- The ez Pick mechanism lets a single-player or single-team entry be submitted at effectively the product of the real legs (ez Pick = 1.0x), subject to the 1.25x minimum entry multiplier.

### Gaps
- No official statement either permits or restricts correlated same-game Player Picks; I found no official per-entry correlation adjustment and only one empirical sample (two legs from one game) suggesting none exists.
- Whether Sleeper blocks specific highly correlated Player Pick pairs in-app (as it does for Combos) is not documented.

## Key Question 4: Typical vig per pick (sum of implied probabilities of both sides) and published analysis

### Takeaway
Sleeper prices each side of each line with its own multiplier; on balanced main lines both sides sit near 1.78x, which sums to about 1.12 implied probability (roughly 12.2-12.6% overround, ~11% hold on balanced two-way action). Real payloads from Sleeper's public lines API (Oct 2026) and an independent measurement ("≈1.78 per side, 12.6% overround"; "overround ≈12.4%") agree. No formal published audit of Sleeper's hold exists; the independent project also reports realized hit-rate x multiplier of 0.89-0.97 per leg across 14,423 Sleeper/Underdog legs.

### Inferences
- Computed overround (1/m_over + 1/m_under) from the cited real pairs: 1.78/1.78 -> 1.1236 (12.4%); 1.83/1.73 -> 1.1245; 1.80/1.77 -> 1.1205; 1.72/1.85 -> 1.1219; 1.82/1.75 -> 1.1209; 1.69/1.88 -> 1.1236; NFL 1.77/1.79 -> 1.1236; 2.03/1.59 -> 1.1215; 5.15/1.07 -> 1.1288 (12.9%); 1.51/2.14 -> 1.1296 (13.0%); the MLB hits 1.72/2.05 pair -> 1.069 (6.9%) is an outlier that may reflect a promo-discounted or stale side. Typical hold on balanced two-way action = 1 − 1/1.1236 ≈ 11.0%; per-leg EV at a fair 50% line paying 1.78 = 0.89.
- For an n-leg Max entry of symmetric 1.78x legs at true 50%, EV ≈ 0.89^n of stake: 0.79 (2 picks), 0.70 (3), 0.63 (4), 0.56 (5), 0.50 (6), 0.39 (8); applying the observed NBA size bonus (x1.08 at 3, x1.10 at 4) gives ≈0.76 and ≈0.69. By comparison a fixed 3x 2-pick (PrizePicks-style) is 0.75, so Sleeper's 2-pick (1.78^2 = 3.17x) is modestly better than a 3x table, and a Sleeper 3-pick (5.64x base, ≈6.09x with bonus) is about even with a 6x table.
- Because the per-leg price is explicit, Sleeper's hold is per-leg multiplicative rather than tabular; the bettor's edge per leg must exceed the ~11% per-leg hold (true p > 1/m), and the n-pick structure does not add a separate parlay tax beyond the product (unlike fixed tables, which compress at higher n).
- The 0.95 relation in the Alphadog MLB note implies Sleeper shaves ~5% of the net return on each leg relative to a reference decimal price (e.g., fair 2.00 -> 1.95); this is lower than the ~11% implied from both-sides overround, which suggests the aggregator reference price already embedded margin. Treat as unresolved.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed or industry audit of Sleeper's hold exists; all numbers are from scraped boards, affiliate examples, or one unverified modeling repo. No source reports Sleeper's blended hold across sports or the hold on alternate lines specifically.
- Reddit threads with payout screenshots were not surfaced by any search (searches returned no r/sleeperapp, r/dfsports or r/sportsbook results on this topic).

## Key Question 5: Lines/multipliers dynamics, "pick_stats" public pick counts, and the public API

### Takeaway
Sleeper's lines and multipliers move before lock (an independent capture shows a line moving 42.5 -> 41.5 with multipliers 1.82/1.75 -> 1.69/1.88 within an hour; scrapers report updates "every few minutes"). Every market in Sleeper's public, unauthenticated `api.sleeper.app/lines/available` payload carries a `pick_stats` object with public over/under pick counts and a popularity score; the app displays the total as a "🔥" counter. One third-party source asserts multipliers "vary with ... the public action on each side"; Sleeper itself does not document its pricing method.

### Inferences
- `pick_stats.counts` gives a direct, free read of the public's side (e.g., 97.7% over on the sampled 75.5 rec-yds line) with a timestamp per line; combined with `payout_multiplier` snapshots this allows testing whether Sleeper reprices against public action (e.g., does the heavily-picked side's multiplier fall?). No source has published that test; the Stokastic claim is unverified.
- Sleeper's board (one line per player-stat, both sides priced, plus an over-only alt ladder with its own multipliers and promo "over_boost" lines) is structurally closer to a sportsbook prop board than to PrizePicks' fixed tables, so standard devig (sum of 1/m) applies per line.
- The §5.19 "market norms" language gives Sleeper a contractual basis to void entries placed on stale or off-market multipliers, which bounds the value of latency/stale-line strategies.

### Gaps
- Sleeper documents nothing about how multipliers are set or when they move; the relationship between `pick_stats` and multiplier movement is untested in any source found.
- The undocumented endpoints (`lines/available`, `available_alt`, `promos`, GraphQL) are third-party observations; Sleeper could change or gate them at any time, and their use is prohibited by Sleeper's Terms (see Key Question 7).

### Inferences
- All boost/free-entry upside is converted to play-through credit with a 30-day clock and a 1x completed-contest requirement; at an ~11% per-leg hold, the expected cost of clearing $X of credit with a single 2-pick entry is roughly 21% of X, so a $100 match is worth roughly $75-80 in expectation if cleared on 2-pick Max entries of fair lines, less if cleared on longer entries.
- Single-athlete "over_boost" lines (e.g., 1.82x -> 2.02x) can flip a balanced leg from EV 0.91 to EV ≈1.01 at true 50%; these are rare (order of 0.1% of lines on the probed days) and are explicitly carved out of the obvious-error clause ("excluding clearly designated promotional offers or enhanced multipliers").
- Ez Picks (1.0x, auto-win) are not a "free square" in the EV sense — they add no multiplier — but Picks on Sale/Slashed Line projections are, and they are limited per promo and cannot be combined with other promos on an Earn-A-Promo qualifying entry.

### Gaps
- Sleeper publishes no cadence for boosts, discounted picks or profit boosts; official terms say "from time to time." The only quantitative frequency data is the API probe (8 promo lines of ~9,100) from one source.
- Exact current affiliate terms differ by code and date (min deposit $10 vs $20; 30-day vs 60-day clearing); no official page states the welcome-offer minimum deposit.

### Inferences
- The combination of §5.18 (per-account entry-fee and multiplier adjustments "for any reason"), experience-tiered pools, and user/sport-specific entry limits gives Sleeper the same practical toolkit sportsbooks use to limit sharp accounts; the absence of public complaints is weak evidence either way given Sleeper's smaller pick'em footprint.
- Using the public lines API for research is technically trivial but a clear ToS breach under §11.1/§11.3 (and placing entries by script breaches §5.20 and Contest T&C §2.7), exposing an account to prize clawback and closure; the arbitration clause with class waiver limits recourse.

### Gaps
- No public evidence (positive or negative) on how often Sleeper actually reduces limits or multipliers for winning accounts, or what triggers "highly-experienced user" classification.
- The former standalone "Paid-Entry Contests of Skill Terms of Use" is no longer published (404), so any pick'em-specific clauses it once contained could not be reviewed; the General ToU §5 now governs.
